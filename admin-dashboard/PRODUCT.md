# Product

## Register

product

## Users
University Administrators and Staff. They use this system in a professional, high-volume environment to manage attendance, schedules, students, and facilities.

## Product Purpose
"Hudour" (Attendance) is a university management and administration dashboard. It exists to provide efficient, practical, and highly functional tools for managing the day-to-day operations of an academic institution. Success is defined by speed, clarity, and ease of completing administrative tasks.

## Brand Personality
Academic, Practical, Authoritative. The tone is strictly professional and function-first.

## Anti-references
- Playful, casual, or "whimsical" interfaces.
- Flashy, trend-driven, or overly stylized designs.
- Consumer-facing SaaS templates with unnecessary decorative elements.
- Anything that sacrifices clarity and function for aesthetics.

## Design Principles
- **Function over form:** Every element must serve a clear administrative purpose.
- **Academic rigor:** The interface should feel structured, organized, and trustworthy, akin to an official university document.
- **High-density clarity:** Support complex data (tables, schedules) without feeling cluttered.
- **Unobtrusive utility:** The design should get out of the user's way and facilitate rapid task completion.
- **RTL First:** Typography, spacing, and layout must respect and optimize for Arabic content readability, even with a left-aligned sidebar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for readability in various lighting conditions (office environments).
- Clear, unambiguous labeling for all actions to prevent administrative errors.
- Respect system preferences for themes (dark/light mode).
